App Name: Financial Markets - الأسواق المالية

Short Description:
Live global exchange rates, cryptocurrency prices, precious metals, and local bank rates.

Full Description:
Astra Tech Financial Markets is your all-in-one companion for monitoring the real-time value of money and precious assets. In a world where prices fluctuate every second, our app provides a clean, fast, and reliable way to track exactly what matters to you.

Features:
• Global Fiat Currencies: Track live exchange rates for USD, EUR, GBP, and dozens of other global currencies.
• Cryptocurrency Tracking: Monitor the real-time market prices of Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), and other top crypto assets.
• Precious Metals: Keep an eye on the live valuation of Gold, Silver, Platinum, and Palladium.
• Local Bank Rates (Egypt): Get the exact buy and sell rates for major local banks directly on your phone.
• Custom Settings & Dark Mode: Tailor the app to your visual preferences with a native Dark Mode and personalized regional settings.

Built by Astra Tech, this application is designed strictly for informational tracking and market transparency. It is entirely free to use and securely fetches data from reliable global endpoints. 

Stay connected to the real value of money with Astra Tech Financial Markets.
